About Our Company

Welcome to Saravana Industries, proudly known as Sengra. We are not just suppliers; we are expert manufacturers with a deep-rooted passion for quality. We specialize in producing all types of high-quality wet grinder stones.

Our mission is simple: to create the most durable, reliable, and efficient grinding stones on the market. We understand that the stone is the heart of any wet grinder, and our commitment to precision ensures you get the perfect grind, every time.

Founded on a principle of quality and reliability, we have over 20 years of experience in the industry. We control the entire manufacturing process, from sourcing the finest raw materials to final inspection. This is the Sengra guarantee of excellence.
